# Matteo Giordani — Premium SEO & Front-End Portfolio Mockup

## ROLE

Act as a Creative Director, Senior Product Designer, Creative Developer and Front-End Engineer.

Create the first high-fidelity visual mockup of:

**Matteo Giordani — SEO Specialist & Front-End Developer**

This is NOT yet the final production website. The goal is to validate the art direction, layout, storytelling, motion language and personal brand before production.

The result must feel:

**Apple-like, premium, minimal, precise, innovative and technically sophisticated.**

It must NOT feel like a generic SEO agency, generic developer portfolio, SaaS landing page, AI template, cyberpunk site, or overly colorful creative portfolio.

---

# 1. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Matteo wants to use the portfolio to:

- present himself professionally;
- attract freelance clients;
- sell SEO and website optimization;
- demonstrate technical SEO and Front-End competence.

Primary market: **Italy**

Secondary language: **English**

Target clients include businesses that depend on organic or local visibility, especially companies, local businesses, restaurants, sport & fitness businesses and SMEs.

The key differentiation is:

> Matteo does not only identify SEO problems. He can also work directly on the website and implement the improvements.

---

# 2. POSITIONING

**Matteo Giordani**  
**SEO Specialist & Front-End Developer**

Primary statement:

> I build websites that rank, perform and convert.

Supporting statement:

> I help businesses get found on Google.

Secondary positioning:

> SEO Specialist for businesses that depend on local and organic visibility.

Do not over-emphasize “Junior”.

Never invent experience, clients, rankings, revenue, awards, testimonials or results.

---

# 3. CENTRAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

> **The website builds itself while you scroll.**

The visitor should feel that a website is progressively being transformed:

```text
DISCOVER
↓
UNDERSTAND
↓
OPTIMIZE
↓
BUILD
↓
PERFORM
↓
CONVERT
```

At the beginning: an unfinished digital product.

At the end: a complete, optimized website.

This should be ONE continuous visual story, not unrelated animated sections.

# 11. VISUAL NARRATIVE

Use these chapters:

| Scene | Visual transformation | Business meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 01 — Discover | Unfinished website appears | A business needs visibility |
| 02 — Understand | Search / structure emerges | Understand how people find the business |
| 03 — Optimize | Technical structure is revealed | SEO and technical improvements |
| 04 — Build | Components and Front-End emerge | Matteo can implement changes |
| 05 — Perform | Interface becomes lighter and faster | Performance and Core Web Vitals |
| 06 — Convert | UX and CTA become clear | Ranking is not enough |
| 07 — Complete | Website becomes a polished product | Rank + perform + convert |

The same visual system should evolve throughout all scenes.

---
